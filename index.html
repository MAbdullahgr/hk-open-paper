The frozen rule (RULE-B). Do not change it while collecting data.

  1. On weekdays at 01:02:00 UTC (06:02 AM Pakistan), compare each futures price with its previous weekday 08:00 UTC price (the Hong Kong close).
  2. If the price is 1.0% or more ABOVE that close: paper SHORT. Otherwise no trade. (Prices 1% or more below are logged as “shadow long” only, to keep checking that the long side loses.)
  3. Paper exits: 01:32:00 UTC (comparison) and 02:00:00 UTC (main).
  4. Fills use the real order book for your chosen $ size, plus 0.10% fees round trip. The point is to measure real slippage.
